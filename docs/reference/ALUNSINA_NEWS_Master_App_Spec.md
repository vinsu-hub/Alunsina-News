# ALUNSINA NEWS: Master App Specification (revision 2026-09-28)

This revision adds, relative to the earlier spec (kept as `ALUNSINA_NEWS_Master_App_Spec.v1.md`):

- **§4 Home:** Daily Briefing Top 5 and the Featured/Lead story are chosen by the **Headline & Briefing Placement Score (§15)**, never editor discretion, never engagement metrics.
- **§7 Story page:** a **Government request banner** (rare, conditional). If a Story has ever been the subject of a government content-removal request, show one small factual banner in the Story Header, with no agency name in the banner itself, just: "This story has been the subject of a government content request. See ALUNSINA's Transparency Report →" (links to the Government Request Log on the Methodology page).
- **§13 Methodology:** must explain how a story qualifies for the Featured/Lead slot or the Daily Briefing Top 5, with an explicit statement that clicks/engagement are never a factor. **Editorial Independence Pledge** (verbatim text below). **Government Request Log**: a table with date, request type, the Story/Source concerned, outcome, and (once an actual request exists) the requesting agency named factually. It feeds the Story banner and appears on this page only.
- **§15 Placement Score:** every active Story is scored at each **12-hour editorial run**. Signals: (1) source count, log-scaled; (2) source-type diversity (how many of the 9 types); (3) regional reach (distinct regions, same region data as Coverage/Blindspots); (4) recency decay on time since last update; (5) open Related Stories chain bump (this is what earns the DEVELOPING tag). **Excluded:** click-through rate, time-on-page, any engagement/virality metric. Equal weighting to start; **log the computed score alongside what actually got placed**; revisit weights after a few weeks of real editions.
- **§17 Open items:** the Editorial Independence Pledge is drafted but **not legally reviewed. Get PH media-law counsel before publishing.** Account system, notifications, RA 10173 contributor data, tip line, and success metrics remain open.

Pledge text (verbatim, publish only after legal review):

> ALUNSINA removes content only in response to valid legal process: a court order, not an informal request. Criticism of a public official is never, by itself, grounds for removal. Where ALUNSINA's Coverage reflects reporting already published by accredited Philippine outlets, taking it down here doesn't erase that reporting. If ALUNSINA ever receives a government request to remove a Story, it is evaluated against this standard and logged publicly in the Government Request Log below, whatever the outcome.

Owner decisions that override spec text: header right side = search · EN · notifications · **For contributors** (no Profile/account); trending ticker = **weekly trending keywords**; mobile tab "Profile" → **Preferences**; X only via official API when a token exists; Magnified News has no reader-engagement signal (no per-region engagement data without accounts).
