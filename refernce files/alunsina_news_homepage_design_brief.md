# Alunsina News — Homepage Design Brief

## 1. Design Direction

Create a **premium editorial news platform** that combines the information-density and source-transparency principles of **Ground News** with the **classic newspaper/editorial aesthetic** of the provided reference.

The interface should feel like a **modern Philippine digital newspaper** rather than a conventional news blog: authoritative, calm, highly structured, information-rich, and easy to scan.

The design should balance:

- **Ground News–style information discovery**
- **Traditional newspaper typography and hierarchy**
- **Editorial magazine-level photography**
- **Source and regional transparency**
- **A clean, restrained interface rather than a highly commercial news portal**

---

## 2. Visual Identity

### Brand

**ALUNSINA NEWS**

Tagline:

> **Truth has more than one source.**  
> *More context. A clearer picture.*

The logo should be presented as a large editorial masthead at the top of the page, similar to a traditional newspaper.

Use a small geometric/spark emblem above the wordmark.

### Overall Mood

**Refined · Investigative · Editorial · Trustworthy · Philippine · Contemporary**

Avoid:

- overly rounded SaaS cards
- excessive gradients
- loud breaking-news colors
- overly colorful dashboards
- generic corporate UI
- excessive shadows

---

# 3. Color System

Use a restrained newspaper palette.

### Primary

- **Warm ivory / paper:** `#F5F3EA`
- **Deep editorial green:** `#123B36`
- **Near-black:** `#17201F`
- **Muted gray:** `#68706D`

### Accent

- **Terracotta / news alert:** `#B95532`
- **Muted gold:** `#B49A62`

### Source colors

Use subtle source-category colors:

- Government — dark green
- National Media — blue
- Regional Media — terracotta
- Independent — purple
- Community — muted teal

Colors should function primarily as **information signals**, not decoration.

---

# 4. Typography

Use a **high-contrast editorial serif** for major headlines and a clean sans-serif for navigation and metadata.

### Headlines

Use something similar to:

- Cormorant Garamond
- Playfair Display
- Libre Baskerville
- Source Serif

Large headlines should feel like a newspaper front page.

### UI / Metadata

Use:

- Inter
- IBM Plex Sans
- Source Sans 3

Metadata should be compact and understated.

# 7. Main Homepage Layout

Use a **three-column editorial layout**.

### Left — Daily Briefing

Approximately **25% width**.

Heading:

**DAILY BRIEFING**

Show 5 stories.

Each item contains:

- Number
- Thumbnail
- Category
- Headline
- Source count
- Region count
- Time

Example:

**01 · GOVERNMENT**

DOJ to file charges vs. former officials in flood control anomaly

`12 sources · view coverage`

This section should feel like a newspaper's **front-page index**.

---

# 8. Center — Lead Story

Approximately **50% width**.

This is the visual focus of the page.

Large landscape photograph.

Overlay:

**DEVELOPING**

Category:

**ENVIRONMENT**

Headline:

> **Metro Manila LGUs prepare for possible flooding as rains persist**

Supporting summary below.

Then metadata:

`18 sources · view coverage · 7 regions · 4 languages`

`Updated 2h ago`

The lead story should dominate visually without feeling like a typical clickbait news site.

---

# 9. Source Coverage Section

Immediately beneath the lead story.

Heading:

**WHAT SOURCES ARE EMPHASIZING**

Small contextual label:

**On: Metro Manila flooding →**

Display four source perspectives horizontally:

### Government

NDRRMC raises alert level in Metro Manila

### Independent

Heavy rains bring flooding in several areas

### Regional

LGUs on full alert for continuous rains

### Community

Motorists face heavy traffic in EDSA

Each card should contain:

- source type
- image
- headline
- 1–2 line summary
- source count
- timestamp

This is one of the most important Ground News-inspired components.

---

# 10. Right Sidebar

Approximately **25% width**.

## Potential Blindspots

Heading:

**POTENTIAL BLINDSPOTS**

Subtitle:

*Areas with limited coverage detected by Alunsina.*

Four categories:

### Geographic
More coverage needed from affected provinces.

### Language
Limited Filipino and regional-language coverage.

### Source
Coverage relies heavily on government statements and a small number of publishers.

### Evidence
Some claims lack primary-source documentation or verifiable data.

Each should have:

- simple outline icon
- title
- short explanation
- `Explore →`

# 16. Ground News Influence

The Ground News influence should come primarily from the **information architecture**, not from copying its visual design.

Important concepts to incorporate:

### Source transparency

Every story should make it easy to understand:

- how many sources cover it
- what regions are covering it
- what languages are represented
- which source categories are covering it

### Coverage comparison

Allow users to see:

**Government vs National Media vs Regional Media vs Independent vs Community**

### Blindspot detection

Highlight stories or regions with:

- limited coverage
- limited language representation
- concentrated sourcing
- insufficient primary evidence

### Regional perspective

Make the Philippines itself a major organizing principle.

Users should be able to move from:

**National → Region → Province → Local**

---

# 17. Newspaper Influence

The second reference image should influence the **visual language**.

Use:

- large serif headlines
- thin horizontal rules
- generous paper-colored backgrounds
- restrained color
- newspaper-like columns
- numbered lists
- editorial photography
- compact metadata
- strong typographic hierarchy

The page should feel almost like a **physical broadsheet translated into a modern digital interface**.

---

# 18. Card Design

Avoid modern UI cards with:

- huge corner radii
- heavy shadows
- floating glass effects

Instead use:

**thin borders + subtle background separation + editorial typography**

Most cards should feel like **sections of a newspaper page**.

---

# 19. Imagery

Photography should feel documentary and editorial.

Prioritize:

- Philippine streets
- government institutions
- people
- communities
- regional landscapes
- environmental stories
- transportation
- education
- agriculture

Images should feel **real, observational, and journalistic**, rather than polished stock photography.

---

# 20. Responsive Behavior

### Desktop

Three-column editorial layout.

### Tablet

Collapse into:

**Left briefing → Main story → Right sidebar**

with some modules becoming horizontal scroll sections.

### Mobile

Priority order:

1. Header
2. Lead story
3. Source coverage
4. Daily briefing
5. Blindspots
6. Philippines coverage
7. My Area
8. Top stories

Use horizontal carousels for dense content rather than shrinking everything.

---

# 21. Core Design Principle

The entire homepage should communicate one idea:

> **Don't just tell me what happened. Show me who is reporting it, where they're reporting it from, what perspectives are missing, and how the story looks across the Philippines.**

The final result should feel like **a premium Philippine newspaper + Ground News-style source intelligence platform**, with **Alunsina News's own visual identity**, rather than a direct copy of either reference.
